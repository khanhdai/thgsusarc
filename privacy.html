Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the TikTok Repost Test Extension (“Extension”) handles information.

Information We Process

The Extension may process TikTok usernames and publicly available video/repost information that is necessary for its testing and analysis features.

Storage

The Extension does not intentionally sell personal information. Information processed by the Extension is used only to provide its requested functionality and testing features. If local browser storage is used, that information remains on the user's device unless the Extension explicitly states otherwise.

Third Parties

The Extension may communicate with TikTok or other services required for its functionality. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to avoid unnecessary collection or retention of information. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Extension's functionality changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Extension developer through the contact information provided with the app.
